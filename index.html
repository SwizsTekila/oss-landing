Questions

Is OSS free?

Yes. Early users get OSS free. Later a few advanced extras will be premium, but logging your training will always be free.

Do I need to be a pro fighter?

No. OSS is for anyone who trains: white belts, hobbyists, amateurs, pros and coaches. If you train, it helps you see your progress.

Does it teach me martial arts?

No, and that's on purpose. Your coach teaches you. OSS logs what you train, tracks your rounds and shows you how you're improving.

Which disciplines does it cover?

MMA, BJJ, Muay Thai, Boxing, Kickboxing, Wrestling and grappling, with timer presets for each.
